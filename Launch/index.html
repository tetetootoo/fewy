Some things are too good to forget.

Fewy is an iPhone quick notes app with a Home Screen widget and a Story maker. Capture the little things, then share the ones worth passing around.

How does the notes widget work?

See recent notes on your Home Screen. Tap the plus button to open the app directly in quick capture.

Can I share my notes as a Story?

Yes. Choose up to three notes from a day, add a title, and place stickers anywhere on the slide. Share the result as a vertical PNG.

What does Studio include?

Studio backgrounds, stickers, and the option to hide the export credit are included for everyone. No purchase is required.

Where are notes stored?

Notes are stored on your device. You decide which notes go into a shared edition.
